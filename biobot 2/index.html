01 · CONSERVATION

Why is conservation important?

Conservation helps species and habitats recover, protects the relationships that sustain ecosystems, and preserves living heritage for future generations.

02 · RECOVERY

What are the effects of conserving?

Protecting habitat and reducing specific threats can help populations recover. It can also support clean water, healthy soils, and communities that share those places.

03 · LOSS

What are the effects of extinction?

Extinction is permanent. It removes a unique branch of life and can change food webs, though the effects depend on the species and ecosystem.

04 · ECOSYSTEMS

What role does each animal play?

Predators, grazers, pollinators, and seed dispersers connect species to one another. Each field note describes a role, while recognizing that nature is complex.
