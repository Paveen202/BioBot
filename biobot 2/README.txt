BioBot — USB copy

OPEN THE APP
1. Copy the entire BioBot folder onto your USB drive.
2. On the computer where you want to use it, open the folder.
3. Open index.html in a recent web browser.

The built-in learning pages, featured species profiles, Q&A, and settings are
included in this folder. An internet connection is needed for the wider GBIF
species search and for online fonts and the featured wildlife photo.

IUCN SPECIES CSV
Import an IUCN Red List CSV from Settings to add its species names on that
computer. Keep a copy of the CSV on the USB drive and import it again on each
computer: imported names and preferences are saved in that browser, not in the
BioBot folder. The included featured species profiles remain available offline.

Keep all files together in the BioBot folder; index.html uses the other files
next to it.
