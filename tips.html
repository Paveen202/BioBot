Small steps, shared impact

50 Tips for Conservation

Practical ways to care for wildlife and the places we share. Start with one that fits your life, then build from there.

Explore the tips ↓

01 — 10

Make space for wildlife

Small choices at home and outdoors can make local habitats safer and more welcoming.

  1. Plant native trees, shrubs, and flowers suited to your region.
  2. Choose a mix of native plants that bloom or fruit in different seasons.
  3. Replace a small part of a lawn with a wildlife-friendly garden.
  4. Leave some fallen leaves in quiet garden spots for insects and soil life.
  5. Avoid pesticides where possible; ask local experts about safer options.
  6. Remove invasive plants using guidance from a trusted local source.
  7. Keep cats indoors or in a secure outdoor enclosure to protect wildlife.
  8. Make large windows easier for birds to see with external markers or screens.
  9. Turn off outdoor lights when they are not needed.
  10. Observe wild animals from a distance and never feed them.

11 — 20

Use energy and water wisely

Using fewer resources can reduce pollution and pressure on natural systems.

  1. Switch off lights when you leave a room.
  2. Choose energy-efficient LED bulbs when replacing old ones.
  3. Walk, cycle, or take public transport when it works for your trip.
  4. Combine errands to reduce the number of car journeys.
  5. Turn off the engine when parked and waiting.
  6. Air-dry clothes when weather and space allow.
  7. Wash full loads of clothes, using a cool setting when suitable.
  8. Fix dripping taps and leaks promptly.
  9. Take shorter showers and turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.
  10. Carry a reusable water bottle or cup.

21 — 30

Reduce waste and pollution

Buying thoughtfully and reusing what you have can keep useful materials out of nature.

  1. Bring a reusable bag when you shop.
  2. Plan purchases so you buy only what you will use.
  3. Repair clothes, toys, and electronics before replacing them.
  4. Use refillable containers for lunches and snacks.
  5. Choose reusable products when they are practical for you.
  6. Compost food scraps if a safe local option is available.
  7. Sort recycling according to your local collection rules.
  8. Take batteries and electronics to an approved collection point.
  9. Put litter in a bin, or join a local cleanup if it is safe to do so.
  10. Avoid releasing balloons or plastic decorations outdoors.

31 — 40

Choose food thoughtfully

Food choices can support healthier soils, lower waste, and responsible fishing and farming.

  1. Plan meals and portions to help prevent food waste.
  2. Store food well and use older ingredients first.
  3. Try more plant-rich meals if that suits your needs.
  4. Choose seasonal produce when it is available and affordable.
  5. Check trusted local guidance when buying seafood.
  6. Avoid products made from threatened wildlife or illegally traded species.
  7. Look for credible sustainability information on wood and paper products.
  8. Support farms and food businesses that share responsible practices.
  9. Grow herbs or vegetables at home or in a community garden if you can.
  10. Freeze or share extra food before it goes to waste.

41 — 50

Help your community protect nature

Conservation grows when people learn, work together, and care for local places.

  1. Follow park rules and leave natural places as you found them.
  2. Stay on marked trails to protect plants and nesting areas.
  3. Keep dogs leashed where local rules or wildlife needs call for it.
  4. Do not move firewood between areas; it can spread pests and disease.
  5. Clean shoes and outdoor gear to avoid carrying seeds between habitats.
  6. Volunteer for a local habitat restoration or planting day.
  7. Support a conservation group or nature center you trust.
  8. Join a reputable community science project and follow its instructions.
  9. Check sources before sharing wildlife or conservation claims.
  10. Encourage local decisions that protect parks, rivers, and wildlife habitat.
